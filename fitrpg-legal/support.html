Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete Account. This removes FitRPG character data for your account. If you also use sibling apps on the same Firebase project, ask us if you need a broader wipe.

Camera / form tracking isn’t counting reps

Give FitRPG Camera access in iOS Settings. Use good lighting, keep your full body in frame, and pick the exercise that matches the move you’re doing.

HealthKit / steps aren’t syncing

Allow Health access for FitRPG in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access. FitRPG only reads selected metrics; it does not write workout samples.

Matchmaking times out or I fight bots

1v1 and 3v3 search for real players first. If nobody is available, the server can fill empty slots with bots so you can still train. Check your network and try again from the Arena tab.

Clan war stuck on “Searching…”

Wars look for another clan for about two minutes, then either pair with a real clan or assign a shadow opponent. Leaders can cancel search from the Clan Wars panel. Pull to refresh or reopen the Clan tab if the UI looks stale.

World Boss says “No active raid”

World bosses are server events. When none is active, wait for the next cycle or check again later. Attacking costs energy — if Attack does nothing, wait for energy to regenerate.

Widgets / notifications

Enable notifications for duel invites and clan war updates. Home Screen widgets use App Group group.com.borisdev.rpg-tracker; if a widget is blank, open the app once after install.
