Support — FitRPG

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Need help with FitRPG?

  • Account / progress: open Profile in the App for Sign In, Privacy, Terms, and Delete Account.
  • HealthKit / camera: grant permissions in iOS Settings → FitRPG if sync or form tracking fails.
  • Multiplayer: ensure a stable network connection; try again if matchmaking times out (bot opponents may fill empty slots).

For App Review or privacy requests, use the developer contact email on the App Store product page.
