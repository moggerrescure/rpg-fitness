Privacy Policy — FitRPG

Last updated: 29 July 2026

FitRPG (“the App”) turns real-world exercise into RPG progress. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account: Firebase anonymous user ID; optional Apple/Google sign-in identifiers and email if you link an account.
  • Profile: username, character class, avatar, in-game stats (XP, gold, equipment, clan membership).
  • Health & fitness: with your permission, HealthKit read access for steps, active energy, and workouts to award in-game rewards. We do not write to HealthKit.
  • Camera: on-device pose estimation for repetition counting. Video is processed on device and is not uploaded for training tracking.
  • Multiplayer: friend lists, battle results, clan data, push notification tokens (FCM).
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and basic analytics (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics) to improve stability.

2. How We Use Data

To run gameplay, sync progress across devices, matchmaking, leaderboards, push notifications, fraud prevention, and App Store compliance (including account deletion).

3. Sharing

We use Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics) under Google’s terms. We do not sell personal data.

4. Health Disclaimer

FitRPG is for entertainment and fitness motivation only. It is not medical advice. Consult a physician before starting any exercise program. Exercise at your own risk.

5. User-Generated Content

Usernames and clan names/descriptions are visible to other players. Do not include personal data of others. We may remove abusive content and suspend accounts that violate our Terms.

6. Retention & Deletion

You may delete your FitRPG account in the App (Profile → Delete Account). This removes your FitRPG character data associated with your account. Contact support if you need help.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under the age required in your region.

8. Contact

Support: see the Support page linked in the App, or email the developer address listed on the App Store listing.
